T﻿he Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick Travel Grant is administered through Duke University and is designed to support in-person research using the Sallie Bingham Center's collection of the [Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick Papers](https://archives.lib.duke.edu/catalog/sedgwickevekosofsky). Sedgwick Travel Grant recipients will be asked to submit a brief report of their grant-supported activities at the conclusion of the grant cycle.

For more details about the application process and timeline, please see the Rubenstein Library's [main travel grant page](https://library.duke.edu/rubenstein/research/grants-and-fellowships). The deadline to apply for a 2026-2027 Travel Grant is February 27, 2026.
