T﻿he Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick Foundation is pleased to announce [it has pledged $15,000 over the next three years to fund the José Esteban Muñoz Award](https://www.gc.cuny.edu/news/gift-honors-friendship-two-queer-studies-pioneers), which was established by the CUNY Graduate Center over a decade ago to honor the legacy of the activist and theorist. Sedgwick, a founder of queer studies and former Graduate Center professor, mentored Muñoz when he was a Ph.D. student at Duke University in the early 1990s, and the two remained close friends throughout their lives. The award was established in 2015 to recognize LGBTQ community leaders for their advocacy and contributions to LGBTQ activism and artistic practice.
