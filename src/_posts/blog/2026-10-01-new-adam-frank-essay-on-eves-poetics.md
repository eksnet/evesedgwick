B﻿uilding on his talk given at the symposium "An Open Mesh of Possibilities: Thinking Through Queerness with Eve Kosofsky Sedgwick's Archive," Adam Frank's new essay, "Bristle, Delicate, Rescue: On Sedgwick's Poetics" has been published in the Duke University journal *Differences*. The essay thinks through Sedgwick's memoir *A Dialogue on Love* to "unfold several aspects of her poetics: an erotics of bristling criticism, an ethics of delicate attention, and a politics of rescue acutely aware of its own destructive impulses and motives." It is available to [read online](https://read.dukeupress.edu/differences/article/37/2/162/412641/Bristle-Delicate-Rescue-On-Sedgwick-s-Poetics?guestAccessKey=79b41ab5-a686-47ae-92d2-af3934b4bc59) for a limited time.
